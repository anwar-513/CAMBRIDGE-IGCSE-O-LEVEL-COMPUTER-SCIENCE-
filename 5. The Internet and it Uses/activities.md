Today, people see the World Wide Web project as one of the greatest inventions in human history because it connects the whole world. Most people view it simply as "the web," a huge, digital library where anyone can instantly find information, watch videos, or chat with friends. Beginners often mix it up with the internet, but tech experts explain that the internet is like the physical roads, while the web is like the cars driving on those roads to deliver information. Instead of being a private tool just for scientists, it is now seen as a free, public space that belongs to everyone and makes everyday life much easier.
